Our solutions

A full portfolio of connected-infrastructure and industrial IoT applications.

  • Smart water metering

    LoRaWAN and NB-IoT connected smart water meters for residential, commercial and bulk supply points.

  • Smart street lighting

    LoRaWAN / NB-IoT connected lighting control and monitoring.

  • Water leak detection

    Acoustic and pressure-based sensors identify leaks early, reducing water loss and repair costs.

  • Water quality monitoring

    Continuous monitoring of pH, turbidity, chlorine and other parameters to safeguard supply quality.

  • Water level monitoring

    Real-time level monitoring in tanks, reservoirs and channels supports optimized management and flood alerts.

  • People & asset tracking

    Location visibility and safety across sites and facilities.

  • ATEX zone sensors

    Certified monitoring for hazardous and explosive-atmosphere environments.

  • Consultancy & RF planning

    Advisory, tender preparation and RF network design from feasibility to go-live.
