Smart street lighting

LoRaWAN / NB-IoT connected lighting control and monitoring.

What the network gives you

A lighting network connected this way is an asset you can manage centrally, not a set of fixtures you only find out about when a resident calls in a fault.

Dimming and switching schedules that used to be fixed at install, or changed fixture by fixture, become something a central platform can adjust across a whole district at once. The same connection that carries a dimming command back also carries power-draw data the other way, which is what makes it possible to tell a failed driver from a lamp that is simply due its scheduled check.

  • Remote control

    Individual or grouped on/off switching and dimming schedules from a central platform.

  • Energy monitoring

    Real-time power consumption tracking to quantify and verify energy savings.

  • Fault detection

    Automatic alerts for lamp failures, driver faults and abnormal power draw.

  • Maintenance planning

    Usage and fault data drive predictive, rather than reactive, maintenance.
