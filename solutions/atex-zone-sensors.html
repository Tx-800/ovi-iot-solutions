ATEX zone sensors

Certified monitoring for hazardous and explosive-atmosphere environments.

What’s monitored

ATEX-rated sensing for the conditions a classified hazardous zone puts on equipment and instrumentation.

A hazardous zone is also the zone where a manual inspection round is hardest to justify sending someone into on a fixed schedule. Sensing that reports continuously, rather than at the next walk-round, moves temperature, vibration and valve status checks from a periodic inspection to something a control room can watch constantly — and catches a developing fault between rounds rather than at the next one.

  • Temperature monitoring

    Continuous temperature sensing on critical equipment to flag overheating before failure.

  • Vibration monitoring

    Vibration analysis on rotating machinery supports early fault and imbalance detection.

  • Valve positioning

    Remote valve position sensing confirms open/closed status for safety and process control.

Where it’s deployed

Built for oil & gas, chemical and process industries operating in classified hazardous (ATEX) zones.
