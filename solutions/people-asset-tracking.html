People & asset tracking

Location visibility and safety across sites and facilities.

What it does

A LoRaWAN tracking layer gives a site one answer to two different questions: where is everyone, and where is everything. The same network that locates a forklift in a warehouse can confirm a lone worker has checked in from a restricted zone, without separate infrastructure for each.

A geofence turns a site map into something the network itself can act on: an alert the moment a tag crosses into a zone it shouldn’t, rather than a manual headcount or a guard relying on a two-way radio. Indoors and outdoors on the same network also means the same dashboard follows an asset from a yard into a warehouse, rather than a handoff between two separate systems.

  • LoRaWAN based indoor and outdoor tracking of personnel across large sites
  • Real-time location and status visibility for tools, equipment and vehicles
  • Geofencing and lone-worker safety alerts for hazardous or restricted zones
  • Deployed across industrial plants, warehouses, campuses and logistics yards
