Water monitoring applications

Water quality monitoring

Sensor-based monitoring across the water network.

Continuous monitoring of pH, turbidity, chlorine and other parameters to safeguard supply quality.

Parameters monitored

Sensors report continuously rather than on the schedule of a manual sample, so a change in supply quality shows up as it happens rather than at the next scheduled test. That matters most exactly when it is hardest to catch manually — a contamination event or a process upset outside normal sampling hours.

A single reading tells an operator what the water looked like at the moment it was sampled; a continuous one tells them what it has been doing since. Watching pH, turbidity and chlorine move together, rather than as separate spot checks, is what turns a quality programme from a compliance record into something that can flag a developing problem before it reaches a customer’s tap.
