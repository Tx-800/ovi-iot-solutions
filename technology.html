Dual connectivity: matched to the application

Selection criteria, not a spec sheet — the choice depends on the deployment.

  • LoRaWAN

    Unlicensed spectrum, private network you own and operate, long range, very low power; suits campus, plant and citywide rollouts where you control the gateways.

  • NB-IoT

    Licensed carrier spectrum, no gateway infrastructure to deploy, deep indoor penetration; suits dispersed assets across an operator’s existing coverage.

OVI selects the connectivity per application rather than forcing one technology across a programme.
